Baseline SOH prediction metrics
===============================

Task: predict later-cycle normalized SOH from earlier cycles of the same battery
Target: normalized SOH
Train/test split: first 70% cycles train, last 30% cycles test

Models:
- naive_last_observed: repeats the last SOH value seen in the training split
- quadratic_polynomial: degree-2 polynomial fitted to discharge_index vs SOH
